## College of Computer Studies Retention & Academic Standing
- **Admission to 3rd Year BSIT / BSCS**: Students must complete all prescribed 1st and 2nd year professional computing courses with an average rating of at least **2.25**. This 2.25 average rating must be maintained.
- **Failing Limit & Shifting**: Any student who fails in more than three (3) subjects shall be advised to shift to another course.
- **CAT Entrance Threshold**: Passing rate of 75% on College Admission Test (CAT) is required for BSIT/BSCS; below 75% enters 2-year ACT and can shift after 1st sem with a 2.25 GPA.
- **Fourth Year Prerequisite Gate**: No student is allowed to enroll in the 4th year unless all Physical Education courses (PATHFit 1, 2, 3, 4) and NSTP (NSTP 11 and 12) have been successfully passed.
- **Theology Requirement**: All candidates graduating from a 4-year program must complete 24 units of Theology (Theo 1a to Theo 4b).

## Official Grading Scale & Equivalents
- **1.00** = 98% and above (Excellent - Summa Cum Laude interval)
- **1.25** = 95% - 97% (Very Good)
- **1.50** = 92% - 94% (Very Good - Magna Cum Laude interval)
- **1.75** = 89% - 91% (Good - Cum Laude interval)
- **2.00** = 86% - 88% (Good - Dean's Lister interval)
- **2.25** = 83% - 85% (Good - CCS 3rd Year Retention Threshold)
- **2.50** = 80% - 82% (Fair)
- **2.75** = 77% - 79% (Fair)
- **3.00** = 75% - 76% (Passed / Minimum Passing Grade)
- **5.00** = Below 75% (Failed)
- **NC** = No Credit (Given when final exam is missed and standing is unsatisfactory; compliance window within 2 weeks after rating sheet submission)
- **W** = Officially Withdrawn (Complied with official withdrawal processing)
- **FW** = Failure due to Unofficial Withdrawal (Stopped attending without official notice)
- **FA** = Failure due to Excessive Absences (Exceeded maximum allowed class absences)

## Attendance, Absences, and Exam Rules
- **Tardiness & Absences**: Arriving 15 minutes late, leaving 15 minutes early, or accumulating 3 consecutive tardiness marks counts as 1 absence.
- **Maximum Absences (FA Drop)**: Incurring 10 absences in full in-person classes (or 5 absences in HyFlex/flexible modality) results in automatic drop with a permanent rating of "FA".
- **Major Examinations**: Administered 4 times per semester: Pre-midterm, Midterm, Prefinal, and Final examinations. Students must present an official Examination Permit issued by the Bursar's / Cashier Office. Special exams must be filed with the Dean within 5 days with valid medical/justifiable proof.
