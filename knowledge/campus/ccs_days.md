## CCS Days & CCS Week Overview & Traditions
- **Definition & Purpose**: The annual **CCS Days** (or **CCS Week**) is the flagship multi-day celebration of the College of Computer Studies (CCS) at Saint Joseph College (SJC). It brings together BSIT, BSCS, and ACT students, faculty, alumni, and student leaders for camaraderie, technology promotion, academic excellence, athletic competition, and cultural entertainment.
- **Event Scope & Atmosphere**: CCS Week is an all-around festival combining high-energy physical sports, esports/online gaming, academic quiz bees, tech launches, and evening social gatherings.
- **Venues Utilized Across Campus & City**:
  - **Cantabo Canticum Novum Mini Theater (CCN)**: Used for solemn tech convocations, opening prayers, professional entrances, keynote addresses, and academic quiz competitions.
  - **Hacbang Gymnasium**: Used for opening Holy Masses, assembly, parade starting points, and the Grand Culmination / Awards Night.
  - **Combado Gymnasium**: Primary arena for physical sports, street yelling, badminton, volleyball, and evening basketball tournaments.
  - **Social Action Center (SAC)**: Venue for board game tournaments and academic breakout competitions.
  - **SJ Garden**: Outdoor campus grounds for traditional Filipino games (*Laro ng Lahi*).

## CCS Week Competitions & Activities Breakdown
- **Esports & Online Gaming Competitions**:
  - Live competitive gaming tournaments featuring popular mobile esports titles, primarily **Call of Duty: Mobile (CODM)** and **Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)**. Usually kicks off on Day 0 or early in the schedule with live broadcasting/viewing.
- **Physical Sports Competitions**:
  - **Basketball**: The marquee athletic event, consisting of team elimination rounds and championship/companionship games under the lights.
  - **Volleyball**: Men's and Women's divisional volleyball tournaments.
  - **Badminton**: Men's Singles, Men's Doubles, Women's Singles, and Women's Doubles brackets.
- **Traditional Games & Board Games**:
  - **Laro ng Lahi**: Traditional Filipino games held at the SJ Garden to foster teamwork and cultural heritage.
  - **Board Games Competition**: Strategic chess and tabletop games hosted at the Social Action Center (SAC).
- **Academic, Tech & Professional Events**:
  - **CCS Quiz Competition**: Fast-paced computing, logic, and general IT knowledge quiz bowl.
  - **Academic Contests**: Programming challenges and academic problem-solving showdowns.
  - **Technology Launches & Industry Certifications**: Formal launching ceremonies for institutional tech initiatives like **Cisco Technology**, **Robotics Technology**, and the **PhilNITS (Philippine National Information Technology Standards)** certification program (including PhilNITS Seal of Excellence and the Blessing of PhilNITS Takers).
- **Pageantry, Culture & Culmination Events**:
  - **Opening Ceremonies & Parade**: Holy Mass, street parade marching through the city/campus, SJC Hymn, and Philippine National Anthem.
  - **Cheering & Pageantry**: Muses and Escorts production number and the Street Yelling competition.
  - **Culmination Night & Variety Show**: Grand runway/fashion showcase, photo opportunities by year level, dinner fellowship, raffle draws, awarding of the **Dean's List**, variety shows, parody competitions, and speeches from the Team Leaders, Department Governor, and Dean.

## CCS Days Standard Schedule & Day-by-Day Flow
- **Day 0 (Esports Kickoff)**:
  - 1:00 PM — Call of Duty: Mobile (CODM) Competition Live
  - 5:00 PM — Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) Competition Live
- **Day 1 (Opening, Pageantry & Sports Day)**:
  - *Morning (Hacbang Gym to Combado Gym)*: 7:00 AM Attendance In $\to$ 7:30 AM Holy Mass $\to$ 8:50 AM Parade to Combado Gym $\to$ 9:45 AM Opening Ceremonies, Welcome Remarks, Inspirational Talk, Team Leaders' Speech, Muses & Escorts Production, Street Yelling Competition $\to$ 11:30 AM Lunch Fellowship.
  - *Afternoon (Combado Gym)*: 12:30 PM Attendance In $\to$ 1:00 PM Badminton Tournament (Men's & Women's Singles/Doubles) $\to$ 3:20 PM Volleyball Tournament (Men's & Women's) $\to$ 6:15 PM Attendance Out & Dinner Break.
  - *Evening (Combado Gym)*: 6:30 PM Attendance In $\to$ 7:00 PM Basketball Elimination Rounds $\to$ 9:00 PM Attendance Out.
- **Day 2 (Tech Convocation, Academics, Laro ng Lahi & Basketball Finals)**:
  - *Morning (CCN)*: 7:30 AM Attendance In $\to$ 8:30 AM Opening Ceremonies & Professional Entrance $\to$ Welcome Remarks $\to$ Cisco Technology, Robotics, and PhilNITS Launchings $\to$ PhilNITS Seal of Excellence & Blessing of Takers $\to$ Closing Remarks.
  - *Afternoon (CCN, SAC, SJ Garden)*: 12:30 PM Attendance In $\to$ 1:30 PM CCS Quiz Competition (CCN), Board Games (SAC), Academic Contest (SAC) $\to$ 3:00 PM Laro ng Lahi (SJ Garden).
  - *Evening (Combado Gym)*: 6:00 PM Attendance In $\to$ 6:30 PM Basketball Championship & Companionship Game $\to$ 9:30 PM Awarding of Basketball Champions, Runner-up & Special Awards $\to$ Attendance Out.
- **Day 3 (Culmination Night & Socials)**:
  - *Afternoon & Evening (Hacbang Gym)*: 4:00 PM Attendance In $\to$ 4:30 PM Opening Ceremonies & Production Number $\to$ Runway Presentation $\to$ Year-level Photo Ops $\to$ 6:00 PM Dinner Fellowship $\to$ Raffle Draw $\to$ Awarding of Dean's List Honorees $\to$ Variety Show & Parody Competition $\to$ Team Awards, Team Leader Speeches, Governor's Speech $\to$ 9:00 PM Closing Remarks.
